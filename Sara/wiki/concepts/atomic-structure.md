# Atomic Structure

## Definition
The internal organization of an atom: a dense positive nucleus (protons + neutrons) surrounded by electrons occupying discrete energy levels (orbitals) described by quantum mechanics.

## Why It Matters
Atomic structure explains: why elements have periodic properties, how and why atoms bond, why reactions release or absorb energy, and why spectral lines occur at specific frequencies. Everything in chemistry flows from this.

## Evolution of Atomic Models

| Model | Who | Key Idea | Fatal Flaw |
|-------|-----|----------|-----------|
| Plum pudding | Thomson (1904) | Electrons embedded in positive sphere | Rutherford's experiment |
| Nuclear | Rutherford (1911) | Dense positive nucleus, electrons orbit | Can't explain stability or spectra |
| Bohr (1913) | Bohr | Quantized orbits, stationary states | Fails for multi-electron atoms |
| Quantum mechanical | Schrödinger (1926) | Probability clouds, wave functions | Current accepted model |

## Critical Numbers to Know

| Particle | Charge | Mass (kg) | Mass (u) |
|---------|--------|-----------|---------|
| Electron | –1e | 9.109×10⁻³¹ | 0.00054 |
| Proton | +1e | 1.673×10⁻²⁷ | 1.00727 |
| Neutron | 0 | 1.675×10⁻²⁷ | 1.00867 |

- Atomic radius ≈ 10⁻¹⁰ m; nuclear radius ≈ 10⁻¹⁵ m (ratio = 10⁵)
- e/m of electron = 1.758×10¹¹ C/kg (J.J. Thomson, 1897)
- Electron charge = 1.602×10⁻¹⁹ C (Millikan oil-drop)

## Bohr Model Formulas (H-atom and H-like ions only)
- Energy: Eₙ = –2.18×10⁻¹⁸ × Z²/n² J = –13.6 × Z²/n² eV
- Radius: rₙ = 0.529 × n²/Z Å
- Velocity: vₙ = 2.18×10⁶ × Z/n m/s
- ΔE = hν = Rн(1/n₁² – 1/n₂²) × hc (where Rн = 1.097×10⁷ m⁻¹)

## Quantum Numbers Summary

| QN | Symbol | Values | Determines |
|----|--------|--------|-----------|
| Principal | n | 1,2,3,... | Shell, energy, size |
| Azimuthal | l | 0 to n–1 | Subshell, shape |
| Magnetic | ml | –l to +l | Orbital orientation |
| Spin | ms | +½, –½ | Electron spin direction |

- Total orbitals in shell n: n²; Total electrons: 2n²
- l: 0=s(1 orbital), 1=p(3), 2=d(5), 3=f(7)
