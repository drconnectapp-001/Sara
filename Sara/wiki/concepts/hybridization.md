# Hybridization

## Definition
The mixing of atomic orbitals of similar energy in the same atom to form a new set of equivalent hybrid orbitals, directed in space to minimize electron repulsion and maximize bond strength.

## Why It Matters
Hybridization explains molecular geometry. Without it, VB theory predicts wrong bond angles for CH₄, NH₃, H₂O. It also determines the number of σ and π bonds a molecule can form.

## Quick Determination Method
**Hybridization = σ bonds + lone pairs on central atom**

| σ bonds + lone pairs | Hybridization | Geometry (no LP) | Bond angle |
|---------------------|--------------|-----------------|-----------|
| 2 | sp | Linear | 180° |
| 3 | sp² | Trigonal planar | 120° |
| 4 | sp³ | Tetrahedral | 109.5° |
| 5 | sp³d | Trigonal bipyramidal | 90°/120° |
| 6 | sp³d² | Octahedral | 90° |

## Key Examples

**sp hybridization:**
- BeCl₂: 2 σ bonds, 0 LP → linear, 180°
- C₂H₂ (acetylene): each C has 1 σ to other C + 1 σ to H = sp; remaining 2p orbitals form 2 π bonds → triple bond

**sp² hybridization:**
- BF₃, BCl₃: 3 σ bonds, 0 LP → trigonal planar, 120°
- C₂H₄ (ethylene): each C has sp², 1 unhybridized p → forms π bond; rotation restricted
- SO₃, NO₃⁻, CO₃²⁻

**sp³ hybridization:**
- CH₄: 4 σ bonds, 0 LP → tetrahedral, 109.5°
- NH₃: 3 σ bonds, 1 LP → pyramidal, 107° (LP pushes bonds closer)
- H₂O: 2 σ bonds, 2 LP → bent, 104.5° (2 LPs compress angle more)

**sp³d hybridization:**
- PCl₅: 5 σ bonds, 0 LP → trigonal bipyramidal; axial bonds (90°) longer than equatorial (120°)

**sp³d² hybridization:**
- SF₆: 6 σ bonds, 0 LP → regular octahedral, all 90°
- IF₇: sp³d³; pentagonal bipyramidal

**dsp² (square planar):**
- [Ni(CN)₄]²⁻, [Pt(Cl)₄]²⁻: d orbital + sp² mixing; 90° bond angles

## Lone Pair Effect on Geometry
Each lone pair reduces bond angle by ~2–2.5°:
- CH₄ (0 LP): 109.5°
- NH₃ (1 LP): 107°
- H₂O (2 LP): 104.5°

## σ vs π bond count
- Single bond: 1σ
- Double bond: 1σ + 1π
- Triple bond: 1σ + 2π
- For molecule: count all bonds; σ = total bonds, π = double bonds + 2×(triple bonds)
