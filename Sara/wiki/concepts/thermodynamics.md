# Thermodynamics

## Definition
The study of energy transformations in chemical systems — how heat is exchanged, whether reactions are spontaneous, and how equilibrium position relates to energy.

## Why It Matters
Thermodynamics answers: "Does this reaction happen?" (spontaneity) and "How much energy does it release/absorb?" (enthalpy). The link ΔG⊖ = –RT ln K connects it directly to equilibrium.

## The Four Laws (Summary)
- **Zeroth:** if A and B are in thermal equilibrium with C, A and B are in equilibrium with each other
- **First:** energy is conserved; ΔU = q + w
- **Second:** entropy of universe increases in any spontaneous process; ΔStotal > 0
- **Third:** entropy of pure perfect crystal = 0 at 0 K

## Key Equations

| Equation | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| ΔU = q + w | First Law: energy conservation |
| w = –pextΔV | Work at constant external pressure |
| ΔH = ΔU + ΔngRT | Relates enthalpy to internal energy for gas reactions |
| ΔH = qp | Enthalpy = heat at constant pressure |
| ΔrH⊖ = Σ ΔfH⊖(products) – Σ ΔfH⊖(reactants) | Hess's Law calculation |
| ΔS = qrev/T | Entropy change |
| ΔG = ΔH – TΔS | Gibbs energy |
| ΔG⊖ = –RT ln K | Links Gibbs energy to equilibrium constant |

## Spontaneity Decision Chart

| ΔH | ΔS | ΔG = ΔH – TΔS | Spontaneous? |
|----|----|---------------|-------------|
| – | + | Always – | Always |
| – | – | – at low T | Low T only |
| + | + | – at high T | High T only |
| + | – | Always + | Never |

## Sign Conventions (IUPAC)
- w is **positive** when work done ON system (compression)
- q is **positive** when heat flows INTO system (endothermic)
- ΔH **negative** = exothermic; ΔH **positive** = endothermic

## Calorimetry
- **Bomb calorimeter:** constant volume → measures ΔU (= qV)
- **Coffee cup calorimeter:** constant pressure → measures ΔH (= qp)
