# Waves

## Definition

A wave is a propagating disturbance that transfers energy and information through a medium (mechanical waves) or through vacuum (electromagnetic waves), without net transport of matter. Every medium element executes SHM about its equilibrium position as the wave passes.

## Why It Matters

Waves is a very high frequency JEE topic (2–3 questions per paper), covering standing waves, beats, and sound speed. It also underlies all of optics and electromagnetic theory in Class 12. The mathematical framework of waves (y = A sin(kx–ωt)) appears everywhere from quantum mechanics to AC circuits.

## Evidence & Examples

**Wave Equation:**
- y(x,t) = A sin(kx – ωt) [travels in +x direction]
- k = 2π/λ; ω = 2πν = 2π/T; v = ω/k = λν

**Wave Speeds:**
- String: v = √(T/μ) [T = tension, μ = linear density]
- Sound in gas: v = √(γP/ρ) = √(γRT/M)
- Sound in air at 0°C ≈ 332 m/s; increases with temperature: v ∝ √T

**Standing Waves (string, fixed both ends):**
- y = 2A sin(kx) cos(ωt)
- Harmonics: νₙ = nv/(2L), n = 1, 2, 3, ...
- Nodes: x = 0, λ/2, λ, ... (fixed points)
- Antinodes: x = λ/4, 3λ/4, ... (maximum amplitude)

**Pipes:**
- Open pipe: all harmonics νₙ = nv/(2L)
- Closed pipe (closed at one end): only odd harmonics νₙ = (2n–1)v/(4L)

**Beats:**
- Beat frequency = |ν₁ – ν₂|
- Amplitude modulation with beat frequency

**Reflection:**
- Rigid (fixed) end: phase reversal (reflected wave inverted)
- Free end: no phase reversal (reflected wave upright)
