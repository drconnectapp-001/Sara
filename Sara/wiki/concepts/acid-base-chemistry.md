# Acid-Base Chemistry

## Definition
The study of proton (H⁺) transfer reactions in aqueous solutions, governed by equilibrium constants (Ka, Kb, Kw) and expressed quantitatively through pH.

## Why It Matters
Acid-base chemistry underlies biochemistry, industrial processes, analytical chemistry, and environmental science. Buffer systems maintain biological pH. Ksp and solubility tie into the same equilibrium framework.

## Three Definitions (Broadest Wins)

| Theory | Acid | Base | Limitation |
|-------|------|------|-----------|
| Arrhenius | gives H⁺ in water | gives OH⁻ in water | Aqueous only |
| Brønsted-Lowry | proton donor | proton acceptor | Can't explain AlCl₃ |
| Lewis | e⁻ pair acceptor | e⁻ pair donor | Broadest; includes metal ions, BF₃ |

## Key Equations

| Expression | Formula |
|-----------|---------|
| pH | –log[H⁺] |
| pOH | –log[OH⁻] |
| Kw | [H⁺][OH⁻] = 10⁻¹⁴ at 25°C; pKw = 14 |
| pH + pOH | = pKw = 14 (at 25°C) |
| Ka expression | [H⁺][A⁻]/[HA] |
| Degree of ionization α | √(Ka/c) for weak acid |
| [H⁺] from weak acid | √(Ka × c) |
| Henderson-Hasselbalch | pH = pKa + log([A⁻]/[HA]) |
| pKa + pKb | = pKw = 14 (conjugate pair) |

## Strong vs Weak

**Strong acids** (complete ionization): HCl, HBr, HI, H₂SO₄, HNO₃, HClO₄
**Strong bases:** NaOH, KOH, LiOH, Ba(OH)₂
**Weak acids (examples with Ka):** CH₃COOH (1.74×10⁻⁵), HCN (4.9×10⁻¹⁰), HF (7.2×10⁻⁴)
**Weak bases:** NH₃ (Kb = 1.77×10⁻⁵), aniline (4.27×10⁻¹⁰)

## Conjugate Acid-Base Pairs
Strong acid → very weak conjugate base (HCl → Cl⁻: essentially no base strength)
Weak acid → stronger conjugate base (CH₃COOH → CH₃COO⁻: significant base)

## Buffer Solutions
**Definition:** resists pH change on addition of small amount of acid/alkali or on dilution

**Acidic buffer:** weak acid + its salt (e.g., CH₃COOH + CH₃COONa; effective at pH ≈ pKa ± 1)
**Basic buffer:** weak base + its salt (e.g., NH₃ + NH₄Cl; effective at pOH ≈ pKb ± 1)

**Henderson-Hasselbalch:**
- pH = pKa + log([salt]/[acid])
- pOH = pKb + log([salt]/[base])

## Salt Hydrolysis

| Salt type | pH | Example |
|-----------|-----|---------|
| Strong acid + strong base | 7 | NaCl |
| Strong acid + weak base | < 7 | NH₄Cl |
| Weak acid + strong base | > 7 | CH₃COONa |
| Weak acid + weak base | ≈ depends on Ka vs Kb | CH₃COONH₄ |

## Solubility Product (Ksp)
- For MₓNᵧ: Ksp = [M]ˣ[N]ʸ
- **Ionic product > Ksp:** precipitation occurs
- **Ionic product < Ksp:** no precipitation; solution unsaturated
- Common ion effect: decreases solubility; shifts equilibrium toward undissolved salt
