# Conic Sections

**Source:** [[raw/Mathematics/kemh111]] | **Date ingested:** 2026-05-12 | **Type:** book-chapter

## Summary

Chapter 11 studies conic sections — curves formed by the intersection of a plane with a double cone. The four conics are circle, parabola, ellipse, and hyperbola. For each, the chapter derives the standard equation, identifies key features (focus, directrix, eccentricity, vertices, axes), and establishes parameter relationships. Conics are essential in both mathematics and physics (orbits, optics, projectile paths).

## Key Points

**Circle:**
- Standard form: (x–h)² + (y–k)² = r² (centre (h,k), radius r)
- General: x²+y²+2gx+2fy+c=0; centre (–g,–f); radius = √(g²+f²–c)
- Condition for circle: coefficient of x² = coefficient of y² and no xy term

**Parabola:**
- Standard: y² = 4ax (axis along x-axis; vertex at origin; focus (a,0); directrix x = –a)
- y² = –4ax: opens left; x² = 4ay: opens up; x² = –4ay: opens down
- Eccentricity e = 1 for all parabolas
- Latus rectum length = 4a; endpoints (a, ±2a)

**Ellipse:**
- Standard: x²/a² + y²/b² = 1 (a > b > 0); a = semi-major axis; b = semi-minor axis
- c² = a² – b²; c = distance from centre to focus; eccentricity e = c/a (0 < e < 1)
- Foci: (±c, 0); Vertices: (±a, 0); Co-vertices: (0, ±b)
- Latus rectum length = 2b²/a
- Sum of distances from any point on ellipse to two foci = 2a (defining property)
- If a² is under y²: major axis is vertical; swap a and b roles

**Hyperbola:**
- Standard: x²/a² – y²/b² = 1
- c² = a² + b²; e = c/a > 1
- Foci (±c, 0); Vertices (±a, 0)
- Asymptotes: y = ±(b/a)x
- Latus rectum = 2b²/a
- Difference of distances from any point to two foci = ±2a (defining property)
- Rectangular hyperbola: xy = c² (asymptotes along coordinate axes)

**Eccentricity Summary:**
- Circle: e = 0; Parabola: e = 1; Ellipse: 0 < e < 1; Hyperbola: e > 1

## My Take

**JEE Frequency: Very High.** Conic sections is one of the highest-weightage Math topics in JEE — 2–4 questions per paper, covering all four conics. Often combined with tangent/normal equations (Class 12).

**Most-asked topics (Class 11 level):**
1. Finding equation of parabola/ellipse/hyperbola from given conditions (focus, directrix, vertex)
2. Identifying the type of conic from equation and finding its parameters
3. Latus rectum calculations
4. Points on ellipse/hyperbola satisfying distance conditions
5. Equation of circle given three points or diameter endpoints

**Common traps:**
- Ellipse: a² under x² means major axis is HORIZONTAL; a² under y² means VERTICAL — always check which is larger
- Hyperbola: c² = a² + b² (NOT a² – b² like ellipse); subtract b²/a² term
- Parabola: y² = 4ax opens RIGHT (positive x direction); y² = –4ax opens LEFT
- Focus of parabola y² = 4ax is at (a, 0) — not (4a, 0)
