# Chapter 2: Structure of Atom

**Source:** [[raw/Chemistry/Part-01/kech102.md]] | **Date ingested:** 2026-05-12 | **Type:** textbook-chapter (NCERT Class 11 Chemistry Part 1)

## Summary
Traces the evolution of atomic models from Thomson's plum pudding to the quantum mechanical model. Covers discovery of sub-atomic particles, Bohr's model and its limitations, and the full quantum mechanical treatment including quantum numbers, orbital shapes, and electronic configuration rules.

## Key Points

### Sub-atomic Particles
- **Electron:** discovered via cathode rays; charge = –1.602×10⁻¹⁹ C; mass = 9.109×10⁻³¹ kg; e/m ratio measured by J.J. Thomson (1897) = 1.758×10¹¹ C/kg; charge by Millikan oil-drop experiment
- **Proton:** discovered by Goldstein (canal rays); charge = +1.602×10⁻¹⁹ C; mass = 1.672×10⁻²⁷ kg
- **Neutron:** discovered by Chadwick; mass ≈ 1.675×10⁻²⁷ kg; charge = 0
- **Thomson's model:** atom = sphere of positive charge with electrons embedded (plum pudding)
- **Rutherford's gold foil experiment:** most α-particles passed through; few deflected; very few bounced back → atom is mostly empty space with tiny dense positive nucleus
- **Rutherford's nuclear model:** positive charge concentrated in nucleus; electrons orbit around nucleus; nuclear radius ≈ 10⁻¹⁵ m; atomic radius ≈ 10⁻¹⁰ m
- **Atomic number (Z):** number of protons; **Mass number (A):** protons + neutrons
- **Isotopes:** same Z, different A (e.g., ¹H, ²H, ³H); same chemical properties
- **Isobars:** same A, different Z (e.g., ⁴⁰₁₈Ar and ⁴⁰₂₀Ca)

### Wave Nature and Quantum Theory
- **Electromagnetic radiation:** transverse waves; speed c = νλ = 3×10⁸ m/s; doesn't need medium
- **Spectrum types:** emission (bright lines), absorption (dark lines on bright background)
- **Planck's quantum theory:** energy is quantized; E = hν; h = 6.626×10⁻³⁴ J·s
- **Photoelectric effect (Einstein):** KE = hν – hν₀ (hν₀ = work function W₀); KE independent of intensity, depends on frequency; proves light is quantized (photons)
- **Bohr's model of hydrogen:**
  - Electrons move in fixed circular orbits (stationary states) — no energy radiated
  - Angular momentum is quantized: mvr = nh/2π
  - Energy of nth orbit: Eₙ = –2.18×10⁻¹⁸/n² J per atom = –13.6/n² eV
  - Radius of nth orbit: rₙ = 0.529n² Å (Bohr radius a₀ = 0.529 Å for n=1)
  - Energy of transition: ΔE = Rн(1/n₁² – 1/n₂²); Rн = 2.18×10⁻¹⁸ J
  - **Hydrogen spectral series:** Lyman (UV, nf=1), Balmer (visible, nf=2), Paschen (IR, nf=3), Brackett (IR, nf=4), Pfund (IR, nf=5)
  - **Limitation:** fails for multi-electron atoms; can't explain splitting of spectral lines (Zeeman/Stark effects)
- **de Broglie's wave-particle duality:** λ = h/mv; applies to all particles
- **Heisenberg's Uncertainty Principle:** Δx·Δp ≥ h/4π; can't know both position and momentum exactly

### Quantum Mechanical Model
- **Wave function (ψ):** Schrödinger equation; |ψ|² = probability density
- **Quantum numbers:**
  - **n (principal):** 1,2,3,... ; determines shell size and energy
  - **l (azimuthal):** 0 to (n–1); determines subshell shape; l=0(s), 1(p), 2(d), 3(f)
  - **ml (magnetic):** –l to +l; determines orbital orientation; number of orbitals = (2l+1)
  - **ms (spin):** +½ or –½; two spin states per orbital
- **Orbital shapes:** s=spherical; p=dumbbell (3 orientations: px,py,pz); d=double dumbbell (5 orbitals); f (7 orbitals)
- **Nodes:** regions of zero probability; radial nodes = n–l–1; angular nodes = l
- **Energy order in H-atom:** 1s<2s=2p<3s=3p=3d<4s=4p=4d=4f
- **Energy order in multi-electron atoms (Aufbau):** 1s<2s<2p<3s<3p<4s<3d<4p<5s<4d<5p...
  - n+l rule: lower (n+l) fills first; if equal (n+l), lower n fills first

### Electronic Configuration Rules
- **Aufbau principle:** fill lowest energy orbital first
- **Pauli's Exclusion Principle:** no two electrons can have same set of all four quantum numbers; max 2 electrons per orbital (opposite spins)
- **Hund's Rule of Maximum Multiplicity:** in degenerate orbitals, electrons fill singly with parallel spins before pairing
- **Anomalous configurations:** Cr = [Ar]3d⁵4s¹ (not 3d⁴4s²); Cu = [Ar]3d¹⁰4s¹ (not 3d⁹4s²) — half-filled and fully-filled d subshells are extra stable
