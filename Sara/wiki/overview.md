## Key Cross-Subject Connections

### Physics ↔ Chemistry
1. **Thermodynamics (shared):** Physics Ch 11 (engines, processes, Carnot) + Chemistry Ch 5 (enthalpy, ΔG, spontaneity) — same First and Second Laws, different applications
2. **Kinetic theory ↔ ideal gas law:** Physics keph205 derives PV=nRT from molecular collisions; Chemistry Ch 5 uses it as given
3. **Mole concept bridges both:** [[concepts/mole-concept]] (Chemistry) → R/Nₐ = k_B appears in every kinetic theory formula (Physics)

### Physics ↔ Mathematics
4. **SHM uses trig:** x = A cos(ωt+φ) — directly uses [[concepts/trigonometric-functions]] identities for KE/PE (double angle formulas)
5. **Wave equation:** y = A sin(kx–ωt) — superposition of two trig functions gives standing wave formula
6. **Calculus in kinematics:** instantaneous velocity = dx/dt; instantaneous acceleration = dv/dt — [[concepts/limits-and-derivatives]] formalize what keph102 introduces informally
7. **Conic sections ↔ orbits:** [[concepts/gravitation]] satellite/planetary orbits are ellipses — [[sources/kemh111]] provides the analytical framework

### Chemistry ↔ Mathematics
8. **Equilibrium constants:** Kc/Kp calculations use logarithms; ΔG° = –RT ln K links algebra and physical chemistry
9. **Probability foundations (Ch 14 Math):** prepares for radioactive decay statistics (Class 12 Physics) and reaction rate theory

## Key Cross-Chapter Connections (Within Chemistry)
1. Bohr model (Ch 2) → ionization energy (Ch 3) → ionic bond formation (Ch 4)

3. Bond enthalpy (Ch 4) → ΔrH calculation (Ch 5) → Hess's Law
